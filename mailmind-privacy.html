MailMind Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MailMind. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we handle the data accessed through our app.

2. Information We Access

MailMind requests read-only access to your Gmail account (via the .../auth/gmail.readonly scope). This access is strictly used to identify emails related to events, tasks, and schedules to build your personalized agenda.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To extract event details and display them in your local app calendar.
  • We do not store your emails on external servers. All data extraction happens securely.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your email data with third parties.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

MailMind's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Your Consent

By using MailMind, you consent to our Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account Security settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: gkhnason@gmail.com
