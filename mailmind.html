MailMind

About the App

MailMind is an intelligent email and agenda management application designed to help you stay organized. It securely connects to your Gmail account to fetch relevant emails and automatically organize your daily events and tasks into a seamless agenda.

Core Features

  • Secure Gmail integration (Read-only access).
  • Automated event and agenda extraction.
  • Local and private processing of your data.
